How it works

Set up in 30 seconds. Use forever.

No sign-up to start. Log in later to sync and connect with friends.

1

Save a place

Search an address or tap directly on the map to pin the spot. Choose from ready-made templates (Bus, Home, Work) or create from scratch with an emoji and a name.

2

Set up alerts

Define up to 5 alerts at different distances, from 500m to 10km. Each alert can vibrate quietly or sound a loud alarm — great for not missing your bus stop while you sleep.

3

Forget and live

Put your phone in your pocket and forget it. Geo Alert monitors in the background with no extra battery drain. When you (or a connected friend) get close, the alert fires — even with the app closed, even on silent.

Features

Simple on the surface.
Powerful underneath.

Built to be simple to use and powerful under the hood.

Up to 5 alerts per place

Each place can have up to 5 alerts at different distances — 500m, 1km, 2km. The first vibrates quietly, the last sounds a loud alarm. No other location reminder app does this.

Alarm that overrides silent

On silent mode, most apps can't alert you. Geo Alert uses the native alarm stream — on iOS and Android — to make sure you'll hear it, even with your phone in your bag on silent.

Connections — notify your people

With login, connect friends and family by phone or email. Get (or send) alerts when someone is arriving — limit of 2 alerts per friend per place.

One-time or recurring

One-time alerts auto-disable after firing — ideal for one-off tasks. Recurring ones work forever, no reconfiguration needed.

Ready-made templates

Bus, home, task — start from a template and customize. Create alerts in 10 seconds.

Smart groups

Organize by Work, Personal, Health, Family and more. Enable/disable groups with one tap.

Alert history

See all alerts that have fired — when, where and what type. Up to 100 records saved.

Optional cloud sync

Sign in with Apple, Google, email or phone to sync your places across devices. Without login, everything stays on your device.

Light and dark theme

Switch between light and dark mode in settings. Your preference is saved.

English and Portuguese

Fully translated interface in EN and PT-BR. Auto-detects your phone language.

FAQ

Questions?


Does it need internet to work?
For local alerts (just for you), your phone's GPS detects arrivals without internet. But to notify your connections or receive notifications from them, an internet connection is required. You also need a connection to search addresses when adding a place.
Does it drain battery?
No. Geo Alert uses the phone's native geofencing system, which is ultra-efficient.
Does it work with the app closed?
Yes! Monitoring runs in the background. Even with the app closed, alerts fire normally.
Does my data go to any server?
Without login: nothing leaves your device — monitoring is 100% local. With login: your places and connections are saved to the cloud. When you arrive at a monitored place, your connections automatically receive a notification. We don't sell or share your data with third parties — it exists only so the app works for you.
How many places can I monitor?
Without login: 3 places with up to 2 alerts each. With login (free): 5 places with up to 3 alerts each, 5 friends and 3 alert subscriptions. PRO: 25 places with up to 5 alerts each, 50 friends and unlimited subscriptions. On iOS, the system also limits to 20 active geofences at once (Apple limitation).
What are Connections?
With login, you can connect friends and family by phone or email and be notified (or notify them) when someone is arriving at a place. Each friend can receive up to 2 alerts per place (from different perimeters). You always decide what to share.
Do I need to pay to use it?
No. Geo Alert is free in both modes (without login and with login). The PRO plan is optional, for those who want unlimited places, more alerts per place and a bigger history.
