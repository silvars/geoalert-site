How it works

3 steps. No hassle.

No sign-up to start. Log in later to sync and connect with friends.

1

Save a place

Search an address or tap the map. Pick an emoji and name it — or use a ready-made template.

2

Set up alerts

Define up to 5 alerts at different distances (500m to 10km). Each one can vibrate or sound a loud alarm.

3

Forget and live

The app monitors in the background. When you (or a connected friend) get close, alerts fire — even with the app closed.

FAQ

Questions?


Does it need internet to work?
No. Monitoring uses your phone's GPS. You only need internet to search addresses when adding a place.
Does it drain battery?
No. Geo Alert uses the phone's native geofencing system, which is ultra-efficient.
Does it work with the app closed?
Yes! Monitoring runs in the background. Even with the app closed, alerts fire normally.
Does my data go to any server?
Without login: nothing leaves your device. With login (Apple, Google, email or phone): your places and connections are synced via Firebase so they work on your other devices. We don't sell or use your data — it exists only so the app works for you.
How many places can I monitor?
Without login: 3 places with up to 2 alerts each. With login (free): 5 places with up to 3 alerts each, 5 friends and 3 alert subscriptions. PRO: 25 places with up to 5 alerts each, 50 friends and unlimited subscriptions. On iOS, the system also limits to 20 active geofences at once (Apple limitation).
What are Connections?
With login, you can connect friends and family by phone or email and be notified (or notify them) when someone is arriving at a place. Each friend can receive up to 2 alerts per place (from different perimeters). You always decide what to share.
Do I need to pay to use it?
No. Geo Alert is free in both modes (without login and with login). The PRO plan is optional, for those who want unlimited places, more alerts per place and a bigger history.
